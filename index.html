Kitwise

Theme documentation · version 0.3.0 · Contact support

Kitwise is a starter theme for small specialist stores: hobby equipment, outdoor gear and similar catalogs. It focuses on clear product information and a short path from browsing to checkout. This guide covers setup, every theme setting, the available sections and how to get help.

1. Getting started

  1. In your Shopify admin, go to Online Store › Themes. Find Kitwise in your theme library and click Customize.
  2. Before editing, use … › Duplicate to keep a backup copy of the theme.
  3. Set your logo, colors and fonts under Theme settings (the gear icon in the editor).
  4. Choose your menus in the Header and Footer sections.
  5. Pick a collection for the homepage Featured collection section and add images to the hero and other sections.
  6. Preview on desktop and mobile using the device toggle at the top of the editor, then click Save and Publish when you are ready.

Empty image settings show Shopify placeholder illustrations. Example cards on the homepage are labelled Example and disappear once you select a collection.

3. Homepage sections

Add, remove and reorder sections in the left sidebar of the theme editor.

Editorial hero

Eyebrow, heading, description, up to two buttons and an image shown in an arched frame. The second button appears only when both its label and link are filled in. Set a focal point on the image (in the image picker or under Content › Files) to control cropping.

Featured collection

Shows products from one collection. Settings: eyebrow, heading, collection, products to show (2–12) and columns on desktop (2–4). Product cards show sale and sold-out badges and a second image on hover when a product has one.

Featured collections

Up to six collection cards. Each block takes a collection and an optional image that replaces the collection's own image.

Image with text

Image, eyebrow, heading, rich text and an optional button. Choose whether the image sits left or right on desktop.

Buying guide

Up to four numbered steps that help beginners choose. Choose the secondary background or the button colors for the section background.

Newsletter

Email signup that adds subscribers to your Shopify customer list with the tag newsletter.

Custom Liquid

For small custom snippets or app embed code. Errors in custom code can break the page, so test on a duplicate theme first.

4. Header and footer

Header: choose the main menu. Kitwise supports three menu levels. On mobile the menu opens as a full-screen panel. Search, account and cart icons are always shown; the cart icon shows the number of items.

Footer: choose the footer menu and write a short description. The footer also shows social icons, country/language selectors (when you sell in more than one market or language), accepted payment icons and the Follow on Shop button.

5. Product pages

Open a product in the editor and select the Product section. Blocks can be reordered or removed: Vendor, Title, Price, Variant picker, Buy buttons, Description, SKU, Buying information (a collapsible row with your own text), Custom Liquid and app blocks.

  • Variant picker: each option value is a button. Unavailable combinations are shown with a dashed outline. Color swatches appear when you set up swatches in your product options.
  • Media: images, videos and 3D models are supported. On desktop, media shows in a grid; on phones, customers swipe or tap thumbnails. Assign an image to each variant so it shows when that variant is selected.
  • Buy buttons: quantity, add to cart, dynamic checkout buttons, Shop Pay Installments messaging, subscription options (from a selling-plan app) and gift card recipient fields.
  • Pickup availability appears automatically for locations with local pickup enabled.
  • Recommendations sections below the product show related or complementary products.

6. Collections, filters and search

Filters come from Shopify's free Search & Discovery app. Install it, then choose filters such as availability, price, product type, vendor or variant options. On desktop, filters show in a sidebar; on phones, under a Filter button. Sorting applies as soon as a customer picks an option.

Predictive search shows matching products, articles and pages as customers type on the search page.

7. Cart and checkout

The cart page lists items with options, subscription plans, discounts and unit prices, a notes field and the order total. The checkout button and accelerated checkout buttons (when enabled in Settings › Payments) take customers to Shopify checkout.

8. Pages, blog and contact

Create pages under Online Store › Pages. Choose the contact template for a contact form. Blog posts use the Blog and Article templates; comments appear when enabled for the blog.

9. FAQ

Why do I see placeholder illustrations?

An image setting is empty. Choose an image in that section's settings.

Why are my homepage product cards labelled "Example"?

No collection is selected in the Featured collection section, or the collection has no products.

Filters don't appear on my collection page.

Install Shopify's Search & Discovery app and add filters there. Filters only show when they have values in the current collection.

How do I change the button and footer color?

Theme settings › Colors › Button background and Button text.

Can I edit the theme code?

Yes, but duplicate the theme first. Code changes are not covered by support, and you may need to reapply them after updating. For custom work, consider hiring a Shopify Partner.

10. Support policy

Support is included with your purchase and covers questions about Kitwise's features and settings, and bug fixes in the theme. We aim to reply within two business days.

Support doesn't include custom code changes, third-party app setup or design services.

To get help, use the Kitwise support form. Please include your store URL, the theme version (shown under Online Store › Themes) and a screenshot or screen recording of the issue.
